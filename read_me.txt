Data supporting the W-B nanocluster structure, adsorption, and reaction-path analyses.

The current NH3 and H adsorption tables are documented in
02_dft_calculations/adsorption/README.md.

The CI-NEB first N-H cleavage dataset is documented in
02_dft_calculations/reaction_path/README.md. That directory contains the
channel-level pathway manifest, cluster-level sampling statistics, all reported
ten-image XYZ trajectories, and the script used to regenerate and validate the
tabulated energies and endpoint labels.
